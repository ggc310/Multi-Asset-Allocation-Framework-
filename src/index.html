        Model portfolio

        The allocation models combined into one set of fund weights, against a reference of global equities (ACWI) and the US Aggregate (AGG). The reference column shows ACWI split into the same country funds by its country weights. Equities start from ACWI’s country weights tilted by the country scores (the Equity allocation settings); then up to the sleeve budget moves into the best-scoring sector, industry, style, factor and regional sleeves, each funded from its own region, so the look-through still rolls up by region. Click an asset class to fold it, or a fund to see its holdings, sectors and role.

              Backtests use only data available at each date and only signals that passed the promotion rule. “Of which timing” is the excess over holding the model’s own average position: most of each edge has come from sitting above neutral in equities or credit on average, not from timing.                     Real assets: backtest

                    Monthly from 2009, gross of costs. The model sleeve holds the real assets scoring above 0.1, weighted by score over three-year volatility with at most 35% in any one, and sits in the Agg when none does; it is compared with holding every asset at equal risk. Managed futures before mid-2019 and copper before late 2011 use proxies (a trend-following construction and the front copper future); Bitcoin enters in 2015.
